# Project: SaaS Portfolio & Business Management System (Internal Tool)
# Version: 1.0.0
# Author: Developer (Self-Project)

## 1. Project Overview
A centralized SaaS dashboard designed to track financial performance (Profit/Loss), project management (Tasks/Clients), and marketing efficiency (Ads Spend). The goal is to have a "Bird's Eye View" of the entire business operation.

---

## 2. Core Functional Modules

### A. Financial Tracking (P&L)
- **Revenue Tracker:** Logs income from different clients/SaaS products.
- **Expense Tracker:** Categorizes costs (Server, API credits, Tools).
- **Ads Management:** Detailed tracking of ad spend across platforms (FB, Google, etc.) vs. conversions.
- **Auto-Calculation:** Real-time calculation of Net Profit (Revenue - Expenses - Ads).

### B. Project & Task Management
- **Task Pipeline:** (To-Do, In Progress, Review, Completed).
- **Client Ledger:** Tracking specific requests for clients (e.g., Client "lklyan").
- **Advance Payments:** Tracking "Avance" or deposits paid by clients.

### C. Analytics & Reporting
- **ROI Dashboard:** Visual representation of Ad Spend vs. Profit.
- **Monthly Reports:** Summary of the most profitable services/prompts.

---

## 3. Technical Requirements (Stack Preferences)
- **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js (for a fast, SEO-friendly dashboard).
- **Backend:** Node.js (Express) or Python (FastAPI).
- **Database:** PostgreSQL or MongoDB.
- **Authentication:** Secure login (JWT) with a logout functionality.
- **UI Framework:** Tailwind CSS with a "Dark Mode" option for a pro look.

---

## 4. Database Schema (Brief)
- **Users Table:** Admin credentials.
- **Finances Table:** `id`, `type` (Income/Expense), `category`, `amount`, `date`, `source`.
- **Tasks Table:** `id`, `client_name`, `task_description`, `status`, `payment_status`.
- **Ads Table:** `id`, `platform`, `spend`, `clicks`, `date`.

---
